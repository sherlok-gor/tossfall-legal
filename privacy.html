TossFALL Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

TossFALL (home screen name: FALL) is a novelty iPhone app that plays a scream and pant sound when the device detects freefall or a high-speed spin. It is published by Szelok Fung.

Data we collect

TossFALL does not create an account, does not include analytics, and does not upload your data to any server.

  • Motion sensors. Accelerometer and gyroscope samples stay on the device and are used only to detect freefall and spin.
  • Microphone. Used only if you choose to record a custom scream or pant. Recordings stay on this iPhone.
  • Files you import. Custom audio files you pick are copied into the app’s private storage on this iPhone. They are not uploaded.
  • Location. If you grant Always location, TossFALL may use significant location changes so iOS can relaunch the app after the system terminates it. Location is not displayed on a map and is not sent anywhere.

Third parties

TossFALL does not share personal data with third parties. Apple may process information as part of App Store distribution and standard device services.

Children

TossFALL is not directed at children under 13. Do not throw a phone near children, on hard surfaces, or from a height.
